# Getting started

You will receive an email or text message from your tradesperson with a link to
My Trade Portal. The link is personal to you and your job, so do not share it.

## Step 1: Open your invitation

Click the link in the message. You will be taken to a secure page that shows the
quote, job, or invoice your tradesperson has shared.

<Note>
If you cannot find the message, check your spam or junk folder. The sender will
be your tradesperson's business name or <strong>noreply@mytradeportal.com</strong>.
</Note>

## Step 2: Confirm your details

The first time you visit, you may be asked to confirm your name and email
address. This makes sure the quote or invoice is connected to the right person.

You do not need to remember a password unless you want to create an account.

## Step 3: Review and act

Once you are signed in, you can:

- Review the quote and choose **Approve** or **Decline**.
- See the status of an ongoing job.
- Pay an invoice.
- Update your contact preferences.

## Signing in later

If you want to come back to My Trade Portal without using the original link, go
to:

```text
https://mytradeportal.com/login
```

Enter the email address your tradesperson used for you. We will send you a
secure sign-in link or a one-time code by email.

<Warning>
Always check that the website address starts with <code>https://mytradeportal.com</code>
before entering your email address.
</Warning>
