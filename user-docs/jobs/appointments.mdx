# Appointments

Your tradesperson will schedule appointments through My Trade Portal. You will
see the date, time, and a description of what will happen.

## How to view your appointment

1. Open the job in My Trade Portal.
2. Go to the **Appointments** section.
3. Select an appointment to see the full details.

## Appointment details

Each appointment shows:

- **Date and time**
- **Estimated duration**
- **What will be done** (for example, first fix, second fix, testing)
- **The address** the tradesperson will attend

## If you need to reschedule

Contact your tradesperson as soon as you can. They can update the appointment in
the system and you will automatically receive the new time.

## If your tradesperson is running late

Your tradesperson can add a note to the appointment to let you know. Check the
job status and messages for updates. If you are worried, use the **Message**
button to contact them directly.
