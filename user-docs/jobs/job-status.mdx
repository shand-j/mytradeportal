# Tracking your job

Once you have approved a quote, it becomes a job. You can track the job status
at any time in My Trade Portal.

## Job statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Booked** | The work is scheduled and waiting for the start date. |
| **In progress** | Your tradesperson has started the work. |
| **Paused** | Work has stopped temporarily, usually for a reason explained in the notes. |
| **Completed** | The work is finished and your tradesperson is preparing the invoice. |
| **Cancelled** | The job has been cancelled. Contact your tradesperson if you are unsure why. |

## What you can see

Open the job to view:

- The original quote and any agreed changes.
- The current status and latest update.
- Appointment dates and times.
- Notes from your tradesperson.
- Photos or documents they have shared (if any).

## Notifications

You will receive an email or text when:

- The job is booked.
- The status changes to **In progress**.
- The job is completed.
- An invoice is ready for payment.

You can turn these notifications on or off in your [notification settings](../account/notifications).
