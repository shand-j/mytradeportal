# Understanding your quote

Quotes can look complicated, but each line is there to help you see exactly
what you are paying for.

## Common terms

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Labour** | The cost of the tradesperson's time and work. |
| **Materials** | Items such as sockets, cables, switches, consumer units, and fittings. |
| **Subtotal** | The total before VAT is added. |
| **VAT** | Value Added Tax. Only shown if your tradesperson is VAT registered. |
| **Total** | The final amount you will pay. |
| **Validity** | How long the quoted price is guaranteed. After this date, the price may change. |

## Why are some items listed by quantity?

Your tradesperson may list items such as:

- 12 x downlights
- 3 x double sockets
- 1 x consumer unit

This shows the number of each item included in the job. If the work changes, the
quote may need to be updated to reflect the new quantities.

## What is a contingency or allowance?

Sometimes a quote includes an estimated amount for work that cannot be priced
exactly until the job starts (for example, chasing cables into walls). Your
tradesperson should explain this in the notes.

## Questions to ask before approving

- Is the price fixed or could it change?
- Does the quote include all parts and materials?
- Is VAT included in the total?
- When will the work take place?
- Is there a guarantee or warranty?

Use the **Message** button on the quote to ask anything that is not clear.
