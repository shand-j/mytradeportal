# Approving or declining a quote

You are never under any obligation to accept a quote. You can approve it,
decline it, or ask your tradesperson to change it first.

## How to approve a quote

1. Open the quote from the email or text link.
2. Review the full breakdown and make sure you are happy with the work and
   price.
3. Click **Approve quote**.
4. Confirm your choice in the pop-up.

Once approved, your tradesperson will be notified and the work will be
scheduled.

<Check>
After approving, you will receive a confirmation email. The quote will move to
your list of active jobs.
</Check>

## How to decline a quote

1. Open the quote.
2. Click **Decline quote**.
3. You can add a short reason if you want to (for example, "too expensive" or
   "no longer needed"). This helps your tradesperson improve future quotes.
4. Confirm your choice.

Declining a quote is free and does not affect your relationship with the
tradesperson.

## What if you change your mind?

- If you decline a quote and then want to go ahead, contact your tradesperson.
  They can send you a fresh quote.
- If you approve a quote and need to change something, message your
tradesperson as soon as possible. They may need to issue a revised quote for any
major changes.
