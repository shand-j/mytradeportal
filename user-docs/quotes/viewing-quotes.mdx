# Viewing your quotes

When your tradesperson has prepared a quote, you will receive an email or text
with a link. Click the link to open the quote in My Trade Portal.

## What you will see

Each quote shows:

- **Your details** — name, address, and contact information.
- **A description** of the work.
- **An itemised list** of materials, labour, and any additional costs.
- **VAT** — shown separately if your tradesperson is VAT registered.
- **The total amount** due.
- **How long the quote is valid for**.

## Navigating a quote

Use the tabs or sections on the quote page to switch between:

1. **Summary** — the headline price and validity date.
2. **Details** — the full breakdown of items and quantities.
3. **Notes** — any extra information from your tradesperson.

## Downloading a PDF

If you need a copy for your records, look for the **Download PDF** button. This
creates a professional, printable copy of the quote that you can save or email to
someone else.

## What to do next

- If the quote looks right, see [Approving a quote](approving-quotes).
- If something is unclear, use the **Message** button to ask your tradesperson a
  question before you decide.
- If you want to compare prices, you can keep the quote open while you check
  other options.
