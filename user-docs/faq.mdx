# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need to download an app?

No. My Trade Portal works in your web browser on any phone, tablet, or computer.
A dedicated customer app is planned for a future release.

## Is it free to use?

Yes, for customers. Your tradesperson pays for the service. You may pay fees to
your card provider or bank as normal.

## What if I lose my invitation link?

You can sign in at `https://mytradeportal.com/login` using the email address your
tradesperson used for you. We will send you a new secure link.

## Can I share a quote with someone else?

Yes. You can download the quote as a PDF and share it. However, the live quote
page and approval buttons are linked to your account.

## What if the price changes after I approve a quote?

Your tradesperson should contact you before any work that changes the price. If
extra work is needed, they may issue a revised quote. You only pay what you have
agreed to.

## How do I know my payment went through?

You will see a success message on the payment screen and receive a confirmation
email. The invoice status will also change to **Paid** in My Trade Portal.

## Can I pay in instalments?

That depends on your tradesperson. If they offer instalments, the invoice will
show the payment schedule. Otherwise, contact them directly to arrange it.

## Why do I need to confirm my email address?

We use your email to send secure sign-in links and important updates about your
job. Confirming it makes sure we are sending information to the right person.

## Can I delete my account?

If you want to delete your account and data, contact your tradesperson. They can
request account closure on your behalf.

## What if I am not happy with the work?

If you have a problem with the quality of work, contact your tradesperson first.
My Trade Portal helps you manage the paperwork, but the work itself is carried
out and guaranteed by your tradesperson.
