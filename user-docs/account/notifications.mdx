# Notifications

My Trade Portal can send you email and text notifications so you always know
what is happening with your job.

## Notification types

You can choose to receive notifications for:

- New quotes
- Quote reminders
- Job status updates
- Appointment reminders
- New invoices
- Invoice payment reminders
- Messages from your tradesperson

## How to change your preferences

1. Sign in to My Trade Portal.
2. Go to **Account** → **Notifications**.
3. Turn each notification type on or off.
4. Click **Save preferences**.

## Email and text messages

You can usually choose between email and SMS for each notification type. If you
do not want to receive texts, make sure you turn off SMS notifications and keep
email notifications on so you do not miss important updates.

## Unsubscribing

You can stop all marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom
of any email. You will still receive transactional emails about your quotes, jobs,
and invoices because they are essential to the service.
