# Your profile

Your profile stores the contact details your tradesperson uses to reach you and
send quotes, invoices, and updates.

## What is in your profile

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Property address

## How to update your details

1. Sign in to My Trade Portal.
2. Go to **Account** or **Profile**.
3. Edit the fields you want to change.
4. Click **Save**.

<Note>
If your email address changes, we will send a confirmation link to the new
address before the update takes effect.
</Note>

## What if your address changes?

If you move house or the work is at a different address, update your profile or
mention it when you message your tradesperson. Make sure the job address is
correct before work starts.

## Multiple properties

If you have properties at different addresses, each one will usually appear as a
separate job or quote. Your tradesperson can help you make sure everything is
linked to the right address.
