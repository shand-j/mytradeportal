# Keeping your account secure

We take security seriously. Here is how to keep your My Trade Portal account safe
and what we do to protect your information.

## How to sign in securely

- Use the secure link we send to your email. You do not need to remember a
  password.
- Never share your sign-in link with anyone else.
- Always check the website address starts with `https://mytradeportal.com`.

## What we protect

- Your personal details (name, email, phone, address).
- Your quotes, jobs, and invoices.
- Your payment information (we never store card details).

## Who can see your information

Only you, your tradesperson, and authorised staff can see your quotes, jobs,
and invoices. Each job is linked to your account and cannot be accessed by other
customers.

## What to do if you suspect a problem

If you receive an unexpected email, see a quote you do not recognise, or think
someone has accessed your account, contact your tradesperson immediately. You can
also email us at **security@mytradeportal.com**.

## Tips

- Keep your email account secure — if someone can access your email, they could
  use your sign-in links.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi to approve quotes or pay invoices unless you trust the
  network.
- Log out if you are using a shared device.
