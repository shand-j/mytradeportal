# Paying an invoice

My Trade Portal lets you pay invoices securely online by card or bank transfer,
depending on what your tradesperson has set up.

## How to pay

1. Open the invoice from the email or text link.
2. Click **Pay now**.
3. Choose your payment method.
4. Enter your payment details securely.
5. Confirm the payment.

<Check>
You will receive a confirmation email and the invoice status will change to
<strong>Paid</strong>.
</Check>

## Payment security

- All payments are processed through a licensed payment provider.
- My Trade Portal does not store your card details.
- The payment page uses HTTPS encryption, just like online banking.

## What if the payment fails?

Check that:

- Your card has not expired.
- You have enough available funds.
- You entered the card number and security code correctly.

If everything looks correct and it still fails, try a different card or contact
your bank.

## Can you pay by bank transfer?

If your tradesperson offers bank transfer, the invoice will show their bank
details. Make sure you include the invoice number as the payment reference so
they can match it to your job.

## Need a receipt?

Once the invoice is paid, the invoice page itself acts as a receipt. You can also
download the PDF for your records.
