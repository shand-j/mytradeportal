# Payment history

You can view all invoices and payments linked to your account in one place.

## How to see your history

1. Sign in to My Trade Portal.
2. Go to **Invoices** or **Payments** from the main menu.
3. Use the filters to show paid, unpaid, or all invoices.

## What each invoice shows

- Invoice number and date
- Job or quote reference
- Total amount
- Amount paid
- Status (Paid, Unpaid, Overdue, Cancelled)

## Downloading old invoices

You can download a PDF of any invoice at any time, even after it has been paid.
Just open the invoice and click **Download PDF**.

## What if something is wrong?

If you believe a payment has been missed or an invoice is incorrect, contact your
tradesperson directly. You can use the **Message** button on the invoice or job
to send them a note.
