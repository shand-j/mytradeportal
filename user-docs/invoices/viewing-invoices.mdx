# Viewing your invoices

When your job is complete, your tradesperson will raise an invoice. You will
receive an email or text with a link to view it.

## What you will see

Each invoice shows:

- Your tradesperson's business details.
- Your details and the invoice address.
- An invoice number and issue date.
- A breakdown of the work and materials.
- The subtotal, VAT, and total amount due.
- The payment due date.

## How to open an invoice

1. Click the link in the email or text.
2. Sign in if you are asked to. We will email you a secure link or code.
3. The invoice opens automatically.

## Downloading a PDF

You can download a PDF copy of any invoice for your records. This is useful for
your own bookkeeping or if you need to share it with a landlord, letting agent,
or accountant.

## What happens if you do not pay on time?

Your tradesperson may add a reminder note or send a follow-up invoice. If you are
having trouble paying, contact them directly to arrange a solution. My Trade
Portal does not add late fees automatically.
