# Welcome to My Trade Portal

My Trade Portal is the easiest way to manage your electrical jobs from quote to
payment. Your tradesperson uses it to send you clear, itemised quotes, schedule
work, and invoice you — all in one place.

You do not need to install anything. Everything runs in your web browser, and you
can use it on a phone, tablet, or computer.

<Note>
The customer portal is being rolled out to trade businesses in phases. If your
tradesperson has invited you, you can use the link they send you to view quotes,
jobs, and invoices. Some features, such as the dedicated customer mobile app,
will arrive in a later release.
</Note>

## What you can do

- **View quotes** sent by your tradesperson and see exactly what you are paying
  for.
- **Approve or decline** a quote with one click.
- **Track jobs** from booking to completion.
- **View appointments** so you know when your tradesperson will arrive.
- **Receive and pay invoices** securely online.
- **Message your tradesperson** and keep a record of every conversation.

## Who this guide is for

This help centre is for **customers** — homeowners and businesses receiving
quotes, jobs, and invoices from a trade business using My Trade Portal.

If you are a tradesperson or admin, you need the [back-office and engineering
documentation](https://github.com/shand-j/mytradeportal/tree/main/docs) instead.
