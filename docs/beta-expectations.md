# Beta Expectations

## What this beta is

The founding beta is a small, free, concierge-led programme for 3–5 known
businesses, with around 10 individual recipients across business owners and
their invited staff. It is intended to exercise real quote, customer, job,
invoice, and support workflows with direct founder assistance.

## What is included

- Current beta features documented in
  [beta-readiness.md](beta-readiness.md).
- Hands-on onboarding and fortnightly founder communication.
- Practical support for setup, quote review, and reporting defects.
- Uniform founding-member terms for every beta participant.

## What is not included

- Paid SaaS subscriptions or Paddle checkout.
- Any promise of launch dates, uptime, data export, or feature delivery.
- Certificates, real offline operation, deposits, accounting integrations,
  BoQ, or operational-alert delivery.
- Per-user pricing, payment concessions, or scope concessions.

## How to participate

Use the product with representative or authorised data, report reproducible
issues, and join scheduled touchpoints. Screenshots and evidence must redact
tokens, passwords, card details, and customer PII.

Feedback is prioritised against beta gates and user impact. We will close the
loop when an item ships or is intentionally not planned; we will not promise
dates for work that is not already committed.
