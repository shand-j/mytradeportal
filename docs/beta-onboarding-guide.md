# Beta Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the My Trade Portal founding beta. This guide describes the first
setup pass and the support route during the beta.

## Before you begin

- Use a TestFlight build supplied by the founder or support team.
- Keep the account email you used for beta invitation.
- Have your business details, logo, working days, and bank details available.
- You do not need a card or Paddle subscription to use the beta.

## 1. Sign in and confirm your business

Open the app, sign in with your invited account, and confirm the tenant
business name, contact details, portal brand colour, and logo.

## 2. Complete payment setup

Bank-transfer details can be completed in Settings. Stripe card payments are
used only where Stripe Connect onboarding has passed and the account is shown
as accepting cards. Do not represent card payment as available if your account
is not connected.

## 3. Add a real work example

Create one representative quote, review the draft, send it to a customer, and
turn it into a job if accepted. Use only test or authorised customer information.

## 4. Complete the invoice loop

Send an invoice for the work, then use bank-transfer reconciliation unless
your Stripe card-payment setup has been verified. Do not use a real card or
customer in live testing without an explicit go-live instruction.

## 5. Learn the support route

- Report bugs and usability problems promptly, with screenshots and no secrets.
- Keep requests concise: what you tried, what you expected, what happened.
- The team may ask for a staging test account or device rather than customer
  data.
- Beta scope is free and hands-on; broad launch availability is not promised.

## Finish

When the first setup pass is complete, tell the founder which quote and job
journey worked, which did not, and whether you need a second onboarding call.
