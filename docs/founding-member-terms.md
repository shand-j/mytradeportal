# Founding-Member Terms — Beta Cohort

> **DRAFT — pending founder approval.** Not yet sent to beta users. Once
> approved it becomes part of the beta comms pack (Delivery Plan item 0.10).

Proposed terms for the ~10 beta users who are helping us build My Trade Portal
before launch.

## The offer

1. **Free full access through the beta period.** Every feature, every tier —
   nothing gated, no card required.
2. **Founding-member launch pricing: 20% off whatever launch prices are set**,
   locked for **12 months from launch**. If launch prices are £25/£39/£69,
   founding members pay £20/£31.20/£55.20.
3. **Your price never increases during the locked period** — if we raise
   prices for new customers in that window, yours stays put.
4. **No card required during beta.** You will never be charged without
   choosing a plan and entering payment details yourself.
5. **30 days' notice before beta ends.** We'll tell you the launch date, the
   final prices, and exactly what your founding-member rate is — a full month
   before anything changes.

## Why these terms

Beta feedback shapes the product; these terms are the thank-you. They also
de-risk the pricing transition: nobody who helped us build gets a surprise
bill, and the 20% / 12-month lock is simple enough to say in one sentence.

## Notes for founder review

- Numbers to confirm: 20% discount depth, 12-month lock length, 30-day notice.
- Applies to all ~10 beta users equally — no per-user negotiation, or the
  terms stop being a policy and start being a liability.
- Grandfathering beyond 12 months is deliberately excluded; review at the end
  of the locked period, not before.
