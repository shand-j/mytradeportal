# Beta Comms Pack

The wave-1 communication pack contains one uniform set of material for every
beta participant. It must not be personalised with negotiated benefits or
different terms.

## Pack

1. [Beta onboarding guide](beta-onboarding-guide.md)
2. [Beta expectations](beta-expectations.md)
3. [Founding-Member Terms](founding-member-terms.md)

## Distribution rule

Send the complete pack to each wave-1 participant before onboarding begins.
Use the same subject, cover note, and document versions for every recipient.
The cover note must say that:

- beta access is free and no card is required;
- the participant should use representative or authorised customer data;
- Stripe card payment is available only after the participant's own setup;
- feedback will be handled through the agreed fortnightly touchpoint;
- the founding terms apply equally and are not individually negotiable.

## Send gate

Do not send the pack until:

- the founding-member terms have founder approval;
- the beta scope and expectations match
  [beta-readiness.md](beta-readiness.md);
- the wave-1 recipient list has been checked against the
  [cohort register](beta-cohort-register.md).

Record every send in the cohort register. A draft, prepared pack, or internal
preview is not evidence that the participant received it.
