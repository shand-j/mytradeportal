# Beta Cohort Comms Pack

This pack is for the first small, free, concierge-led beta cohort. Send the
same pack to every cohort member; do not negotiate individual exceptions.
Founder approval of the founding-member terms is required before sending.

## Onboarding guide

1. Install the current TestFlight build using the link supplied in the email.
2. Create the business account and complete the onboarding checks.
3. Add the tenant's public details and logo.
4. Create a quote, review the AI draft, edit it as needed and send it to a test
   customer.
5. Convert the accepted quote to a job and try scheduling.
6. Create an invoice; during beta use bank transfer unless Stripe card payments
   have passed their live gate for the account.
7. Report bugs, unclear copy and missing data in the named feedback channel.
   Do not send real card details, passwords or customer secrets in reports.

## What beta means

- Access is free during the documented beta period; no card is required.
- Paddle checkout and paid SaaS subscriptions are not enabled.
- The product is iOS/TestFlight-first and may change weekly.
- The electrician reviews and remains responsible for every AI-assisted quote.
- Card payments are available only after the account and live Stripe gates
  pass; bank transfer is always an explicitly manual alternative.
- Support is concierge-led. Response time and maintenance windows are not
  contractual service levels.

## Beta feedback and privacy

Feedback may include bug details and app events. Quote descriptions, photos,
customer details and payment metadata are processed only for the product and
support purposes described in the privacy notice. Remove unnecessary personal
data before adding screenshots or test data to issue reports.

## Uniform founding-member terms

Attach or link `founding-member-terms.md` unchanged. The terms provide 20%
off launch prices for 12 months, protection from price rises during that lock,
and at least 30 days' notice before the beta ends. There is no individual
negotiation or extended grandfathering.

## Cohort send record

Record only the minimum operational evidence: recipient, send date, comms
version/date and delivery result. Do not store secrets or customer PII in the
cohort register.
