# Friday One-Pager

Six lines, every week, same order — familiarity is the point. One page, no
jargon without a gloss, every number links to its dashboard. The same artefact
becomes the stakeholder update and the investor update — write once.

Source: [Stakeholder Communication & Documentation](mytradeportal-research/Stakeholder-Communication-and-Documentation.md) §2.

## Template

```text
Week ending: YYYY-MM-DD

1. AI spend vs budget — £X of £600 monthly budget; on track / over / under.
2. Unit economics — p50 £X / p95 £X AI cost per org; IER X:1 ▲▼►.
3. Quality — keep-rate X% (trend); what the worst drafts had in common.
4. Adoption — signup → first AI draft → sent quote funnel; trial conversions.
5. Shipped this week — ≤3 bullets, in user language ("customers can now…").
6. Decisions needed / made — what we need from stakeholders, or what was decided and why.
```
